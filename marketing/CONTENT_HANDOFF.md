# StudyOS Content Handoff

## Product

StudyOS is a free-only learning product with study planning/progress, problem solving, review, and AI-assisted study surfaces. New app checkout is disabled. Historical billing references are not a current offer.

## Demo And Screenshots

Use the real Production routes as the source for product screenshots:

- `/demo` for the demo entry
- `/demo/dashboard` for sample dashboard hierarchy

Keep the `DEMO MODE` context visible where sample student data appears. Demo metrics and conversations are illustrative sample state, not customer outcomes or live AI evidence. Do not include authenticated customer data in published captures.

## Visual Identity

Calm, focused, academic, useful. Preserve the existing StudyOS layout, readable surfaces, and restrained motion. `MIDNIGHT STUDY WEEK` uses a subtle navy/night accent and moon/stars; it must remain secondary to study actions, optional, reduced-motion friendly, and absent after its scheduled end.

## Prohibited Claims

- Fabricated users, testimonials, scores, improvement percentages, retention, or success rates.
- Causal claims that StudyOS or the event raises grades or guarantees results.
- Claims that demo activity is real user activity or that a demo response proves a production AI request.
- Current PRO/PREMIUM pricing, subscription availability, or checkout availability.
- Unverified claims about legal compliance, personal-data retention, international transfers, processors, payouts, or merchant status.
